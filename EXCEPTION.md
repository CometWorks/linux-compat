## 0. Definitions and scope.

AGPL terms retain their AGPL meanings. "Grantors" means copyright holders applying
this exception, solely in respect of material they are entitled to license.
"Covered Implementation" means the Program's AGPL-covered implementation code,
excluding expressly permissively licensed material and separately licensed
third-party software. Permissive licensing of an SDK does not license the
implementation which it calls.

"Supported Interface" means an interface designated for third-party use in the
applicable upstream CometWorks project's published SDK or interface documentation
for the version used, including its documented callbacks, types and operations.
Public visibility, technical accessibility or designation solely by a downstream
fork does not suffice. The Program's internal execution of a documented operation
is not unsupported access by its caller.

An "Independent Extension" incorporates or adapts no Covered Implementation in a
manner requiring copyright permission, and interacts with Covered Implementation,
directly or through intermediaries, only through Supported Interfaces and their
documented operation, without an arrangement excluded by section 2. Separately
permissively licensed material may be used under its own terms. The extension's
own interfaces and dealings with separately licensed game components or third-party
services are unrestricted by this definition. Same-process execution, documented
API data exchange, or dependence on the Program for useful execution does not
by itself disqualify an extension.

## 1. Permission for qualifying extensions.

Notwithstanding AGPL conditions which would otherwise require the combination to
be licensed as a whole under the AGPL, you may link or combine the Program with
an Independent Extension, run the combination, permit remote interaction and
convey it. You may license the extension under terms of your choice, including
proprietary terms, provided you comply with the AGPL for covered parts and this
exception where relied upon.

Solely because of that qualifying combination or interaction, the extension need
not be AGPL-licensed or included in the Program's Corresponding Source. This
permission covers qualifying server, UI and companion plugins, client mods and
external services, supplied together or separately, without a fee, registration
or separate agreement. It does not permit proprietary licensing of Covered
Implementation.

## 2. Exclusions and anti-evasion.

Section 1 does not exempt:

a) Copied, adapted, translated, decompiled or otherwise transformed Covered
Implementation requiring copyright permission.

b) Internal runtime patches, method replacements, detours, IL rewriting,
reflection into internal state, or access to undocumented implementation details
outside Supported Interfaces.

c) Wrappers, proxies, private protocols or modified hosts providing that
unsupported access or patching on another component's behalf.

d) Covered modifications divided among plugins, assemblies, processes,
repositories or services.

Eligibility depends on the work and its actual interaction, including through
intermediaries. Plugin labels, permissive wrappers, network transports, patch
size, the proportion of original code or unchanged files on disk do not establish
independence. Ordinary adapters confined to Supported Interfaces and replacements
expressly provided by such interfaces remain eligible. Functionality resembling
a core feature does not alone establish copying or adaptation.

Exclusion limits this additional permission; it does not itself establish that
a work is covered, expand copyright protection or restrict independently
available AGPL, permissive-license or statutory rights.
